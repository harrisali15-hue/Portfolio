PROJECT TEMPLATE
================
1. Copy everything between the two lines below.
2. Open index.html and find "ADD NEW PROJECTS HERE".
3. Paste it at the TOP of the list, right after the "[" (newest first).
4. Fill in your own text. Keep the comma after the closing "}" so the next project still works.
5. Save and push to GitHub.

- link: your project's LIVE site address (what visitors will open and copy).
- tags: as many as you like, e.g. ["JavaScript", "HTML", "Supabase", "Next.js"].
- The project's icon appears on its own if its site has an Apple touch icon. Nothing to add.

------------------------------------------------------------
  {
    title: "PROJECT NAME",
    description: "DESCRIPTION",
    link: "https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO/",
    tags: ["TAG 1", "TAG 2", "TAG 3"]
  },
------------------------------------------------------------
